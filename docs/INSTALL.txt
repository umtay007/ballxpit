BALLxPIT Online Co-op 0.1.9
===========================
Play sparrow's "BALLxPIT: Local Coop" with a friend who is anywhere. Only you (the host) need the
game and the mods. Your friend opens a link in their web browser, sees your screen, hears the game
and controls Player 2 with keyboard and mouse or a controller.

Windows only.


INSTALL (host only)
-------------------
The easy way: double-click Install-BALLxPIT-OnlineCoop.bat. It finds the game through Steam
(or asks for the folder), downloads BepInEx if the game doesn't have it yet, puts the files below
in place and sets older copies aside as .old. Then carry on with step 4. If Windows says "Windows
protected your PC", click "More info" and "Run anyway". The steps below do the same by hand.

1. Close the game.
2. BepInEx 6, the mod loader (skip if your game already runs BepInEx 6; BepInEx 5 does not work
   with BALL x PIT). Download
       https://builds.bepinex.dev/projects/bepinex_be/788/BepInEx-Unity.IL2CPP-win-x64-6.0.0-be.788%2B5b766a3.zip
   and extract everything in it into the BALL x PIT game folder, the one with BALLxPIT.exe (for
   example E:\SteamLibrary\steamapps\common\BALLxPIT).
   Careful: Windows' "Extract All" suggests a new folder named after the zip. Remove that part of
   the path. Afterwards these must sit right next to BALLxPIT.exe:
       winhttp.dll   doorstop_config.ini   dotnet\   BepInEx\
3. Extract this zip into the same game folder, again without an extra folder, and allow it to
   overwrite files. It adds:
       BepInEx\plugins\BALLxPITLocalCoop.dll
           sparrow's Local Coop 0.1.0 plus a few small hooks for the online part. It replaces the
           original. Replace it together with the other DLL whenever you update.
       BepInEx\plugins\BALLxPITOnlineCoop\BALLxPITOnlineCoop.dll
           The online part.
       BepInEx\config\BepInEx.cfg
           Turns off a BepInEx feature that doesn't work in Unity 6 games like this one.
   If an old BALLxPITLocalCoop.dll lives somewhere else under BepInEx\plugins, delete it. (If you
   forget, BepInEx still picks the new one because its version is higher.)
(If you have BALLxPITOnlineCoop-with-BepInEx.zip, it already contains step 2: just extract it.)

4. Start the game. The FIRST start takes a few minutes: a black BepInEx window with scrolling text
   opens and BepInEx prepares the game (it downloads some Unity files, so stay online). Don't close
   that window; closing it closes the game. Later starts are quick.
5. In the main menu an "Online Co-op (F8)" button appears in the top-right corner, and when you
   start a run a second player (Local Coop's P2) stands next to you. If not, see
   "If something's off" below.


HOST A GAME
-----------
1. Press F8 (or click the "Online Co-op (F8)" button) and click "Host an online game".
2. Wait a few seconds for a line like
       Internet link: https://some-random-words.trycloudflare.com/#K7QM2P  (click to copy)
   The first time, the mod downloads cloudflared.exe (Cloudflare's free tunnel program, about
   40 MB) and Cisco's OpenH264 video encoder (0.5 MB) into BepInEx/plugins/BALLxPITOnlineCoop.
   Click the line to copy the link.
3. Send the link to your friend (Discord, text, anything). They open it in Chrome, Edge or
   Firefox, type a name and click "Join as Player 2". The code after # is the password, so only
   people you send the link to can get in.
4. Start a run. Your friend now controls P2. You still do the menus, your own level-up picks and
   pausing; your friend picks P2's upgrades on their page.

Other links the panel can show:
  Direct link       http://your-ip:47777/...  Offered when your router accepts UPnP. A little less
                    lag than the internet link, but browsers only allow H.264 video and
                    controllers on https links, so this one sends the bigger JPEG pictures.
  Same Wi-Fi link   For someone in your house.
Windows may ask whether BALLxPIT may accept connections: allow it for the direct and Wi-Fi links.
The internet link works either way.

More people can open the link while P2 is taken; they watch, and get a "Play as P2" button when
the spot frees up. Use "Kick P2", "New code" and "Stop hosting" in the F8 panel.


P2'S OWN HEALTH
---------------
P2 has its own health, shown above P2 and on your friend's page. When it runs out, P2 is knocked
out for 20 seconds (can't move, shoot or be hit), then gets back up with half its health. If YOU
run out, the run ends as usual. When you heal, P2 heals by the same amount. This works for local
co-op too. Settings are under [Co-op] in BepInEx/config/BALLxPITOnlineCoop.cfg: SeparateHealth,
DownedSeconds, ReviveHealthPercent, SharedHealing.


P2'S OWN BALLS, LEVEL-UPS AND FUSERS
------------------------------------
P2 has its own balls, passives and stats. At the start of a run P2 gets copies of what you start
with. After that, whenever the team levels up (XP is shared), you pick your upgrade on the game's
level-up screen as usual and P2 gets a pick of its own: a new ball, a ball upgrade, a new passive or
a passive upgrade. Your friend picks at the bottom of their page. If nobody is on the page (local
co-op, or your friend is busy), the F8 panel shows P2's choices and you can click one for P2. The
panel also lists P2's balls and passives, and "LV UP" appears above P2 while a pick is waiting.
When a fuser is picked up, you use it on the game's screen as usual, and once that screen closes P2
gets a fuser of its own ("FUSER" above P2): Fission (1 to 5 random upgrade levels for P2's things),
or one of the Fusions and Evolutions P2's own balls allow. What P2 got pops up on your friend's
page and in the panel.
Things to know:
- P2's balls follow P2's passives and stats (damage, crit, speed, max health, ...), yours follow
  yours.
- P2's pick is applied once your own level-up or fuser screen is closed.
- Quitting and continuing a run later starts P2 over with copies of your current balls.
Turn it off with SeparateLoadout = false under [Co-op] in the config; P2 then uses copies of your
balls, as in plain Local Coop.


PLAYING ON TWO PCS (WORK IN PROGRESS): THE SYNC TEST
----------------------------------------------------
The plan for friends who both own the game: each PC runs the whole game and only the players'
controls travel over the internet. That only works if the game plays out exactly the same way on
both PCs when it gets the same start and the same controls. The sync test finds out. It doesn't
connect anything yet; it tells us whether the approach can work, and if not, what goes wrong.

For each test run the mod makes every frame exactly 1/60 s long, fixes the run's random seed,
starts the game's clock at an agreed time, puts P2 on its exact starting spot, and plays for you
(stand still, aim up, keep shooting). Don't touch mouse, keyboard or controller during a run. It
stops after a minute, at your first level-up, or if you die or pause. Recordings from older
versions don't work with this one: record again.

1. F8 > "Playing on two PCs" > "1. Record a test run". Start a new run (any character, any
   level) from the menu, or restart the one you're in, and wait. It saves
   BepInEx\sync-test-recording.json.
2. "2. Check against the recording", then start or restart the SAME character and level. The
   panel says IN SYNC, or OUT OF SYNC with the moment and what differed.
3. Your friend installs the same version, puts your sync-test-recording.json in their BepInEx
   folder and does step 2 on their PC.
Send me BepInEx\sync-test-result.txt from each check (and your friend's), whatever it says. If
it says "restart the game and check again", the checking game had been running for longer than
the recording one: restart it and check right away.
Different unlocks and upgrades in your saves will show up as "Setup:" lines; that's expected for
now and useful to know.


YOUR FRIEND'S CONTROLS
----------------------
  Move                WASD or arrow keys, or the left stick
  Aim                 point with the mouse, the right stick, or hold J / L to turn
  Shoot               hold the left mouse button or Space, or RT / A
  Auto-shoot on/off   E or right-click, or Y
  Pick a level-up     1 / 2 / 3 or click, or D-pad left/right then X
  Full screen         F        Sound on/off   M        Help   H


IF SOMETHING'S OFF
------------------
- No black BepInEx window on start, and no BepInEx\LogOutput.log afterwards: BepInEx isn't
  running. winhttp.dll and doorstop_config.ini must be right next to BALLxPIT.exe, not in a
  subfolder.
- No "Online Co-op (F8)" button in the main menu, and no second player in runs: the mods aren't
  loading. Check the two DLL paths above and that there's no extra BepInEx folder one level down,
  such as BALLxPIT\BALLxPITOnlineCoop\BepInEx. BepInEx\LogOutput.log says what went wrong.
- The button is there but F8 does nothing: some keyboards need Fn+F8, and other programs can take
  F8. Click the button instead, or pick another key with PanelKey in the config.
- "Still connecting to Cloudflare": a firewall or antivirus is blocking cloudflared.exe. Allow it,
  or use the direct link.
- The panel says your Local Coop DLL is the original: the old BALLxPITLocalCoop.dll is still being
  loaded. Delete it and keep the one from this zip.
- Choppy or blurry picture: look at the "Picture" line in the F8 panel.
    "H.264 ... Mbit/s" is the good case: the mod finds the fastest rate the connection holds by
      itself (up to MaxBitrateKbps in the config, 6000 by default).
    "JPEG" means your friend's browser didn't take H.264: use the internet (https) link in Chrome,
      Edge or Firefox. The brackets say why if the encoder itself is missing.
  Your upload speed is the limit either way. On slow connections the mod also sends the sound at
  half quality. BepInEx/LogOutput.log gets a "Stream:" line every 20 seconds; send it if the
  picture stays bad.
- Picture upside down for your friend: they press H and tick "Flip it".
- No game sound for your friend: the F8 panel has a "Sound:" line saying where it comes from.
  Set StreamAudio = false if the sound capture causes any trouble for you.
- Game stutters for you while hosting: lower MaxFps in the config.
- P2 never gets level-up picks, or P2's balls act strangely: set SeparateLoadout = false and send
  BepInEx/LogOutput.log (look for lines starting with "P2").
Everything the mod does is logged in BepInEx/LogOutput.log.

Settings live in BepInEx/config/BALLxPITOnlineCoop.cfg (created on first start).
